01 · Biography

I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Computing & Information Sciences, Rochester Institute of Technology, USA, in 2026, advised by Dr. Yunbo Zhang. Before that I received an M.S. in the Department of Computer Science at RIT in August 2021, advised by Dr. Yu Kong, and a B.S. from the School of Science, Qingdao University of Technology, China, in July 2019.

My research interests encompass human-robot collaboration, open-set recognition, robot learning, AR-based teleoperation systems, tactile sensing, and their applications in computer vision tasks. I believe that multi-dimensional interaction with intelligent robots is the future.
